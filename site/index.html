Travel guide
Travelling to Türkiye for treatment

 

  
Passport & entry
Entry rules depend on your nationality. For example, British citizens can stay without a visa for up to 90 days in any 180-day period, with a passport valid for at least 150 days. Please check with the Turkish embassy before you travel.
Flights
İstanbul, Ankara, İzmir and Antalya all have international airports. Your surgeon decides when it is safe to fly home.
Accommodation
Hotels close to your hospital, for you and a companion.
What to bring
All medical reports and imaging, your medicine list and enough of your regular medicines, and loose, comfortable clothing.
Insurance
Standard travel insurance often excludes planned treatment. Ask your insurer about cover abroad.
Time & language
Türkiye is on GMT+3 all year. Your consultant helps you communicate at the hospital.

FAQ
Questions patients often ask

 
Is the first assessment free?
Yes. Reviewing your request and explaining your options costs nothing.
What is the difference between Option A and Option B?
Option A includes treatment, flights, accommodation and transfers. Option B is the treatment price only; travel services can be added.
How is the final price decided?
Each hospital prepares a personalised quote from your file. The final price is confirmed after your physician examines you in person.
Who gives the second opinion?
A licensed specialist physician reviews your records and writes the report. A video call is arranged only if the physician considers it necessary.
Can a family member come with me?
Yes, and we recommend it for surgery. Option A can include your companion's flights and accommodation.
Why should my photos not show my face?
To protect your personal data, only the area to be treated should be visible. For facial procedures, your consultant arranges a separate secure way to share photos, only with your consent.
What are your working hours?
Seven days a week, 08:30–21:00 Türkiye time (GMT+3).
Is the first second opinion really free?
Yes. Your first second-opinion request is free. We confirm what is included when we reply to you.
