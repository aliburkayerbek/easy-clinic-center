About us

Health in Türkiye, made easy

Easy Clinic Center was founded on 1 October 2020 with one mission: to make a difference through pioneering practice and to become a world health brand, guided by ethical principles, a distinguished team and service that meets international quality standards.

We are your independent coordinator with Türkiye's leading hospitals. We understand your medical situation, find the right specialist, obtain transparent hospital-based prices and organise every step of your journey.

At our partner hospitals, treatment is delivered to international standards, supported by advanced imaging and laboratory services. We treat your health as we would treat our own.

Transparency

Every price comes from the hospital itself. You see what is included before you decide.

Medical integrity

Decisions belong to you and your physician. We never push a procedure.

Personal care

One named consultant follows your file, seven days a week.

Privacy

Your medical data is shared only with the physicians involved, and only with your consent.

How the second opinion works

Chronic disease follow-up centre

Specialists review your results at regular intervals, adjust your plan and send you clear reports while you stay at home. It works alongside your own doctor, not instead of them.

Suitable for

  • Diabetes, high blood pressure, heart disease
  • Thyroid, kidney and lung conditions
  • Every 3 months: results review
  • Every 6 months: medication review
  • Every year: full report

Typical times that vary from patient to patient. A minimum stay may be required before flying; your physician confirms your plan after examining you. Cosmetic procedures are for adults (18+) only.

AI-supported second opinion

A specialist's opinion, without travelling

We put your case to a specialist physician and report the answer back to you in writing. Lengthy one-to-one appointments are not needed; a video call is arranged only if the physician considers it necessary.

  1. 1

    Upload

    Reports, imaging (CD/DICOM), lab results and your current treatment plan.

  2. 2

    AI-assisted summary

    Our system organises your files and lists the key clinical questions.

  3. 3

    Specialist review

    A specialist physician reviews the summary together with your original files.

  4. 4

    Written report

    You receive the evaluation and treatment options in plain language.

What AI does: organises and translates documents, summarises your history and highlights what the specialist needs to see.

What AI does not do: diagnose or recommend treatment. Every medical opinion is written by a licensed specialist physician.

Travel guide

Travelling to Türkiye for treatment

Passport & entry

Entry rules depend on your nationality. For example, British citizens can stay without a visa for up to 90 days in any 180-day period, with a passport valid for at least 150 days. Please check with the Turkish embassy before you travel.

Flights

İstanbul, Ankara, İzmir and Antalya all have international airports. Your surgeon decides when it is safe to fly home.

Accommodation

Hotels close to your hospital, for you and a companion.

What to bring

All medical reports and imaging, your medicine list and enough of your regular medicines, and loose, comfortable clothing.

Insurance

Standard travel insurance often excludes planned treatment. Ask your insurer about cover abroad.

Time & language

Türkiye is on GMT+3 all year. Your consultant helps you communicate at the hospital.

UK patients: read the NHS guidance on going abroad for treatment

FAQ

Questions patients often ask

Is the first assessment free?

Yes. Reviewing your request and explaining your options costs nothing.

What is the difference between Option A and Option B?

Option A includes treatment, flights, accommodation and transfers. Option B is the treatment price only; travel services can be added.

How is the final price decided?

Each hospital prepares a personalised quote from your file. The final price is confirmed after your physician examines you in person.

Does AI make medical decisions?

No. AI organises and summarises your documents. Every medical opinion is given by a specialist physician.

Can a family member come with me?

Yes, and we recommend it for surgery. Option A can include your companion's flights and accommodation.

Why should my photos not show my face?

To protect your personal data, only the area to be treated should be visible. For facial procedures, your consultant arranges a separate secure way to share photos, only with your consent.

What are your working hours?

Seven days a week, 08:30–21:00 Türkiye time (GMT+3).
