About us

Last updated 2026-10-31

Easy Clinic Center is a personal health consultancy based in Istanbul, Türkiye. We were founded on 1 October 2020 to help patients, many of them in the United Kingdom, get a clear second medical opinion or arrange treatment at partner hospitals in Türkiye, with every step explained in writing.

What we do

  • Second medical opinion. You send your question and your records. A specialist physician reviews them and gives you a written report. Second opinions start from $30.
  • Treatment in Türkiye. We coordinate treatment at partner hospitals: plastic, reconstructive and aesthetic surgery, general surgery, dental treatment and internal medicine. You receive written hospital quotes in two forms: Option A, all-inclusive (treatment, flights, accommodation and transfers), or Option B, treatment only, with travel arranged by you.
  • Practical help. Optional flights, accommodation and transfers, language support and follow-up after you return home.

What we are not

We are a consultancy, not a hospital. We do not treat patients ourselves. Treatment is provided by our partner hospitals, which carry the clinical responsibility for it. A second opinion is a physician's review of the records you send; it does not replace a physical examination or emergency care. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

How a request works

1. You send a request through the form on our home page. It takes about two minutes. 2. We reply by e-mail. If more information is needed, such as reports, scans or photos, we ask you for it by e-mail. Please do not send photos that show your face. 3. You receive the answer in writing: a physician's report for a second opinion, or written hospital quotes for treatment.

Partner hospitals

We work with 14 private and university hospitals and centres in Istanbul, Ankara, İzmir and Antalya. They are independent providers and their names are listed in the Hospitals section of our home page. A hospital appearing in our directory does not mean that every physician working there has a personal agreement with us.
